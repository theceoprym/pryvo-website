01Log your nutrition

Log meals without
typing for ages.

Scan the barcode, take a photo of your plate, describe the meal in the chat or search for the food by hand. PRYVO adds up calories and macros for the whole day.

  1. a

    Barcode

    Scan the packaging and take over the nutrition facts.

  2. b

    Photo

    Photograph your plate. The AI estimates calories and macros, you check the amounts and adjust them.

  3. c

    Chat

    Type “200 g chicken with rice” and PRYVO logs it for breakfast, lunch, dinner or a snack.

  4. d

    Manual

    Search for a food and set the amount yourself.

AI results are estimates. Please check them, especially if you have allergies or intolerances.

06FAQ

Questions,
briefly answered.

What can PRYVO do?

PRYVO brings nutrition and training into one app. You log meals by barcode, photo, chat or by hand, train in Gym, Calisthenics or Running and see how your weight and goals develop.

Is PRYVO free?

Yes. PRYVO is free to download. Searching foods, logging by barcode or by hand, using recipes and training work without a subscription. AI features are included with limits, for example one photo and ten chat entries per day.

What does Premium offer?

The weekly plan with shopping list and dishes to swap, recognition of up to 20 meals per day by photo, Panda Sensei and the AI coach for training and nutrition, plus form check, run and progress analyses. Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription; you see the price in the App Store before you buy.

Is PRYVO available for Android?

PRYVO is currently available for iPhone. The Android version for Google Play is coming soon.

How does logging by photo work?

You take a photo of your plate, the AI recognises the meal and estimates calories and macros. The result is an estimate: check the amounts and adjust them if needed, especially if you have allergies or intolerances. One photo per day is included for free, up to 20 with Premium.

Which sports are available?

Currently Gym, Calisthenics and Running. PRYVO records runs via GPS including the route.

Is PRYVO a medical device?

No. PRYVO is not a medical device and does not replace medical advice. Please note: the app itself is currently in German.
